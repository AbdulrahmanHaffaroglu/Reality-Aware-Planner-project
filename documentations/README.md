# The Idea

A tool that helps you grow in a few chosen topics each month by letting you plan
your week in advance, log it in under 30 seconds a day, and review what really
happened versus what you expected.

The goal is not a perfect routine. It is to stay in the game when the week doesn't
go as planned.

## Core Rules

- **Max 3-4 active topics.** If you want to add a new one, you must pause another. This forces real sacrifice decisions instead of planning everything and failing at all of it.
- **Every topic has two numbers:**
  - **Target:** what you normally aim for per week (hours)
  - **Bare minimum:** the least that still counts as "I didn't drop it"
- **Minimums must be able to reach the monthly goal.** If your weekly minimums add
  up to less than the monthly goal, the app warns you when you plan.
- **Hours measure effort, not learning.** The reports show time spent, and that's all they claim to show.
- **Missing log days.** No log for a day is treated as "unkown day" in the review and the user will still be asked to do it even if briefly, if he doesn't remember it the program will try to estimate it

## Daily Log (under 30 seconds)

At the end of the day, enter:

1. Minutes spent per topic (skip the ones you didn't touch)
2. goals of the week that was accomplished in that day.
3. **tags for the day:** went out, tired, busy, slacked, sick, good day, etc.
4. Optional: minutes spent on **time-wasters**, using categories you define yourself

## Unplanned vs Wasted Time

Two separate categories, defined by you:

- **Unplanned:** things that took your time but you don't regret (friends, family, errands)
- **Wasted:** things you decided are time-wasters (games, scrolling, etc.)

## Weekly Review

At the end of each week the app shows, per topic:

- Target vs bare minimum vs actual
- Whether you hit the minimum, hit the target, or missed both
- The most common tag of the week
- Total unplanned and wasted time

Then it asks you to plan next week, using your **real completion rate** from past
weeks as a guide (for example, if you plan 20h but usually do 13h, it suggests
planning closer to 13h).

## Monthly Report

- Hours spent per topic vs monthly goal
- How your weekly expectations changed compared to reality
- Which tags appeared most, and on which weekdays you lose the most time
- Where your time went: goals, unplanned, wasted, and unaccounted for
